# Community code of conduct

Vecta contributors and maintainers should make participation safe and respectful.
Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, respect identities
and privacy, and welcome constructive questions and correction.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing,
publication of private recordings/credentials or deliberate disruption are not
acceptable in project issues, reviews, discussions or project-related events.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate material, ask for correction, temporarily
restrict participation or ban repeated/severe misconduct. Enforcement should be
proportionate, explain the applicable rule where safe, and protect reporter privacy.
Conflicts involving a maintainer should be reviewed by another uninvolved person
when available. Avoid retaliating against good-faith reports.

The owner must designate and verify a private community-reporting contact before
opening public community venues. Until then, use an already trusted private owner
channel; do not post sensitive incidents in a public issue. This document does not
invent a staffed contact or promise confidentiality a public forum cannot provide.
Security defects should follow SECURITY.md's private vulnerability-reporting route.
